Case 03Our project

We built a hacking case for AIs to solve

A fake game studio on a fake computer. A crime happened. The AI must find who, how, and the two clues that prove it, with screenshots, a mouse and 60 clicks.

Case 777 · The leaked betaClicks 2 of 60
  1. 01A big AI practisesOpus, Sonnet and Sol each play 20 practice cases.
  2. 02It writes the playbookOne chapter per crime. General lessons only, no names, no answers.
  3. 03Small AIs get the playbook and playHaiku and Luna play 55 new cases, alone and with it.
  4. 04Every game is checkedOnly the game's own clicks count.

Three lessons from the playbook's leak chapter.

  • Follow the leaked copy's watermark back to the exact download.
  • Put every clock in one time zone before comparing times.
  • A fake login page alone doesn't prove phishing: check that a password was sent.

Case 04The incidents

Three crimes at the studio

Each one can happen three ways. The AI must find who did it, how, and the two records that prove it.

Crime 1 · The leaked beta

An unreleased build of the game is downloaded with a stolen login and shows up on a forum.

  1. A stolen login
  2. The beta is downloaded
  3. It appears on a forum

Three ways it can happen

  1. 1A colleague's written-down password
  2. 2A fake email that asks for the password
  3. 3A password reused from an old breach

Crime 2 · The fake invoice

The supplier's bank account is swapped for a thief's. The next invoice is paid to the wrong account.

  1. "Please update our bank details"
  2. The account is changed
  3. The invoice is paid to a thief

Three ways it can happen

  1. 1An insider in finance
  2. 2A look-alike supplier address
  3. 3Login prompts spammed at night until one is approved

Crime 3 · The missing prototype

The only handheld prototype is on its shelf on Friday and gone on Monday. Door badges and logs tell how it left.

  1. Locked hardware room
  2. Someone opens the door
  3. The unit leaves the building

Three ways it can happen

  1. 1In an employee's bag
  2. 2Handed to a courier after a fake pickup request
  3. 3A visitor who walked in behind staff

Case 06Guard duty · a side test

Can a playbook stop scams before they happen?

A small extra test on top of the main one. A new game: 8 requests arrive each day, some are scams. Stop every scam without blocking real work. A day is safe if all 8 are right. The playbook here is a short one written for this game.
