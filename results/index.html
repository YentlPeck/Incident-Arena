OUR PROJECT

We built a hacking case for AIs to solve.

Pixelforge is a made-up game studio on a fake computer. Something secret has leaked. The AI must find out who did it, how they got in, and which two clues prove it. It only gets screenshots and a mouse, like a person, and at most 60 clicks.

CASE #777 · THE LEAKED BETACLICKS: 2
  1. 1A big AI practisesClaude Opus, Claude Sonnet and GPT-6.1-Sol each play the same 20 practice cases.
  2. 2It writes a playbookAfter each case it is told what really happened and writes down general lessons. No names, no answers.
  3. 3Small AIs get the playbookClaude Haiku and GPT-6-Luna play 55 brand-new cases: once alone, once with the playbook.
  4. 4Every game is checkedOnly games played with nothing but the game's own clicks count. Rule breakers are played again.

A computer program makes a new random case every time, with new names, clues, false leads and traps. The same tricks as in the news above.

  • Follow the leaked copy's watermark back to the exact download.
  • Put every clock in one time zone before comparing times.
  • A fake login page alone doesn't prove phishing: check that a password was actually sent.
